Privacy Policy – TrueCheck

Last updated: April 2026

TrueCheck is a browser extension that allows users to verify information, translate text, and get context from selected text on web pages.

1. Information We Collect

TrueCheck does not collect personal data. It only processes text selected by the user.

2. Use of Information

Selected text is used solely to generate responses such as verification, information, translations, and meanings.

3. Sharing

Selected text may be sent to AI services such as OpenAI in order to process requests.

4. Storage

We do not store personal data. Only basic preferences such as language settings may be stored locally in the browser.

5. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information.
